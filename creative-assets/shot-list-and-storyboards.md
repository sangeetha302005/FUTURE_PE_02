## 1. Production Overview & Safe Zones

When shooting vertical video (9:16 aspect ratio: `1080 x 1920 px`), framing must respect mobile platform UI safe zones to avoid text overlays being obscured by platform interaction icons (likes, comments, audio tags, profile handles).

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [TOP SAFE MARGIN: 150px]                               │
│ - Free of text overlays (Status bar & search icons)    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                        │
│               OPTIMAL VISUAL FOCUS AREA                │
│                                                        │
│   • Eye-line positioned at 60% vertical height         │
│   • On-screen text overlays positioned in center 50%   │
│   • Macro product pump demonstrations in center        │
│                                                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [RIGHT SAFE MARGIN: 120px]                             │
│ - Right side reserved for like, comment & share icons  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [BOTTOM SAFE MARGIN: 280px]                            │
│ - Bottom area reserved for caption, audio & CTA button │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 4. Production Equipment & Setup Checklist

- **Camera/Phone:** iPhone 13/14/15 Pro or Samsung Galaxy S22/23/24 Ultra set to 4K 60fps or 1080p 60fps (ProRes / HDR turned off for standard SDR color grading).
- **Lighting:**
  - Key Light: 18-inch LED Ring Light or softbox at 45 degrees, color temp 5500K.
  - Fill Light: Soft natural window light on opposite side.
- **Audio Gear:** Wireless lavalier microphone (DJI Mic, Rode Wireless GO II, or Boya Wireless) clipped discreetly inside collar or lapel.
- **Props Required:**
  1. 2x Clean, fingerprint-free bottles of GlowUp Daily Hydrating Moisturizer.
  2. 1x Pack of standard blue oil-blotting sheets (Clean & Clear or generic).
  3. Clean bathroom counter or minimalist wooden/stone vanity surface.
  4. Neutral morning wardrobe (soft beige, white, sage green, or muted tones).
