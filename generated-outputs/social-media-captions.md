## 1. Caption Strategy Overview

Social media captions are not mere afterthoughts; in short-form video algorithms, captions serve three critical functions:
1. **The First-Line Hook:** Expanding upon the video's visual hook to keep scrollers reading and watching longer (increasing dwell time).
2. **Context & Clarity:** Delivering concise ingredient and texture proof for viewers who want quick information.
3. **Frictionless Action Cue:** Guiding the viewer to tap the profile link without aggressive, off-putting sales pressure.

Below are four tailored caption variations engineered for different audience attention spans and algorithmic objectives.

## 3. Curated Hashtag Architecture

To avoid hashtag penalties and maximize organic reach across high-intent search feeds, hashtags are organized into a strict three-tier hierarchy (totaling 10 relevant tags):

| Tier | Category | Selected Hashtags | Strategy & Algorithmic Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Broad High-Volume Tags | `#SkincareRoutine` `#DailyMoisturizer` `#CleanBeauty` | Places content in broad category discovery feeds with high monthly browse volumes. |
| **Tier 2** | Specific Niche & Problem Tags | `#LightweightHydration` `#NoWhiteCast` `#NonGreasy` `#HealthySkinBarrier` `#SkincareCommunity` | Captures high-intent users actively searching for solutions to greasy skin or white cast. |
| **Tier 3** | Branded & Campaign Tags | `#GlowUpSkincare` `#GlowUpDaily` | Builds branded search equity and aggregates creator UGC under one trackable tag. |

## 4. Compliance & Quality Guidelines
- **Anti-Spam Verification:** Zero irrelevant trending tags (e.g., `#fyp`, `#viral`, `#trending`).
- **No Fabricated Reviews:** Captions feature observational benefit descriptions, never invented quotes or unverified clinical percentages.
- **Accessibility:** High-contrast emoji bullets break up dense text, improving screen-reader and mobile readability.
