> **Engine:** PromptAdsCraft AI Multi-Variable Prompt Synthesis Engine  

---

## 1. Executive CTA Strategy

A Call to Action (CTA) in short-form video advertising must match the viewer's psychological readiness. Aggressive "BUY NOW" commands on top-of-funnel awareness videos create cognitive resistance, while vague "Check it out" prompts on paid retargeting ads bleed ad spend without driving conversion.

For GlowUp Daily Hydrating Moisturizer, CTAs are organized across four intent categories, anchored by an objective-aligned primary recommendation and backed by a strict anti-deception compliance audit.

---

## 2. Recommended Primary Call to Action

- **Recommended Primary Line:**  
  `"Tap the link in bio to discover your new daily essential."`
- **Target Funnel Placement:** Spoken during the final 3 seconds (`0:27–0:30`) paired with a high-contrast end card graphic.
- **Conversion Rationale:** Reduces purchase anxiety for first-time viewers while clearly signaling where to find ingredient details, skin-type suitability, and ordering options.

## 4. Compliance & Consumer Protection Verification

- **Deceptive Urgency Check:** 0 instances of artificial countdowns, fabricated stock counts, or false rush mechanics.
- **Discount Verification:** Promotional discount CTAs are locked behind explicit user confirmation.
- **Friction Assessment:** CTAs clearly indicate whether clicking leads to an informational landing page or an immediate checkout, preventing bait-and-switch friction.
