# Customer Pain Points — GlowUp Skincare Campaign

---

## Overview

Understanding the specific frustrations of the target audience is foundational to writing effective UGC advertisements. Every hook, script, and CTA in this campaign is rooted in one or more of the pain points documented below.

---

## 1. Documented Customer Pain Points

### Pain Point 1: Heavy, Greasy Moisturizers
**What it is:** Many moisturizers — particularly cream-based formulas — leave a thick, oily film on the skin that feels uncomfortable and takes a long time to absorb.

**Emotional impact:** Frustration, avoidance of moisturizing altogether, discomfort during the day.

**How GlowUp addresses it:** Lightweight, fast-absorbing formula that disappears within seconds of application.

**Advertising angle:** *"I stopped moisturizing for months because every product left me looking like I dipped my face in olive oil."*

---

### Pain Point 2: White Cast on Skin
**What it is:** Many moisturizers (and SPF-containing products) leave a visible white or ashy residue that is particularly noticeable on medium-to-deep skin tones.

**Emotional impact:** Reduced confidence, frustration for darker-skinned users who feel excluded by mainstream skincare.

**How GlowUp addresses it:** No white cast formula; invisible finish on all skin tones.

**Advertising angle:** *"Finally a moisturizer that actually disappears — no white cast, no chalky mess."*

---

### Pain Point 3: Complicated, Multi-Step Skincare Routines
**What it is:** Skincare content often promotes elaborate routines with 5–12 separate products, creating overwhelm for people who simply want healthy-looking skin.

**Emotional impact:** Overwhelm, inconsistency (skipping moisturizing because it feels like too much effort), decision fatigue.

**How GlowUp addresses it:** One simple, effective product that replaces the need for multiple moisturizing steps.

**Advertising angle:** *"I don't have time for a 10-step routine. I have three minutes and one product that handles it."*

---

### Pain Point 4: Skincare That Causes Breakouts
**What it is:** Consumers with sensitive or acne-prone skin often experience breakouts from products containing synthetic fragrances, heavy oils, or harsh preservatives.

**Emotional impact:** Distrust, damaged confidence, desire for clean and safe ingredients.

**How GlowUp addresses it:** Natural, plant-based ingredient formulation designed to work with skin, not against it.

**Advertising angle:** *"I've broken out from more moisturizers than I can count. Clean ingredients changed everything."*

---

### Pain Point 5: Wasted Money on Overhyped Products
**What it is:** Consumers frequently purchase expensive skincare products based on marketing promises that don't deliver real-world results.

**Emotional impact:** Financial frustration, skepticism toward skincare brands, reluctance to try new products.

**How GlowUp addresses it:** Mid-range accessible price point; honest, straightforward product positioning without exaggerated claims.

**Advertising angle:** *"I spent a small fortune on serums and creams before finding something simple that actually works."*

---

### Pain Point 6: Oily Skin That Looks Worse After Moisturizing
**What it is:** People with naturally oily skin often avoid moisturizers because rich formulas make their skin shinier and increase sebum production.

**Emotional impact:** Misconception that oily skin doesn't need moisture; frustration and product avoidance.

**How GlowUp addresses it:** Non-greasy formula specifically designed to hydrate without increasing oiliness — suitable for oily skin types.

**Advertising angle:** *"Oily skin people — yes, you still need to moisturize. And no, it won't make things worse."*
