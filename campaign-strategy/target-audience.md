# Target Audience Analysis — GlowUp Skincare Campaign

---

## 1. Primary Audience Profile

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **Age Range** | 22–38 years old |
| **Gender** | Primarily female; gender-neutral product |
| **Location** | Urban and suburban India, Tier 1 & Tier 2 cities |
| **Education** | Graduate and above |
| **Occupation** | Working professionals, students, young parents |
| **Income Level** | Mid-income, willing to invest in accessible self-care |
| **Device Preference** | Mobile-first; primarily scrolls on Instagram and YouTube |

---

## 2. Psychographic Profile

### Values & Priorities
- Values **simplicity and efficiency** — wants a morning routine that takes under 5 minutes
- Prioritises **clean, transparent ingredient labels** over brand hype
- Believes skincare should **work for everyone**, regardless of skin type or tone
- Interested in self-care but **not obsessed** with 10-step Korean routines
- Seeks **honest, relatable recommendations** from real creators over polished brand campaigns

### Motivations
- Desire to feel confident and put-together without investing significant time
- Curiosity about affordable alternatives to expensive skincare brands
- Interest in switching from products that have caused greasiness or breakouts
- Peer influence from skincare content creators and UGC recommendations

### Digital Behaviour
- Spends 45–90 minutes daily on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
- Discovers products primarily through UGC content, creator recommendations, and comment sections
- Reads reviews before purchasing; trusts peer opinions over brand advertising
- Makes purchasing decisions quickly when a product clearly solves a recognisable problem

---

## 4. Customer Journey Map

```
AWARENESS
  └─ Scrolls past a relatable UGC Reel or Short
  └─ Hook captures attention in first 2 seconds

INTEREST
  └─ Creator describes a pain point the viewer recognises
  └─ Viewer stays to see the product introduction

CONSIDERATION
  └─ Sees product benefits demonstrated naturally
  └─ Reads captions and comments for social proof

INTENT
  └─ Clicks "Learn More" or visits product page
  └─ Searches for more reviews

ACTION
  └─ Purchases or adds to wishlist
```

---

## 5. Audience Insight Statement

> "This audience wants skincare that respects their time, works with their skin type, and doesn't come with a complicated instruction manual. They are skeptical of bold brand claims but respond well to honest, creator-style storytelling that shows — rather than tells — what a product can do."

|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | Discovery and sharing | High — primary platform for skincare UGC |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Educational and demonstration content | Medium-High |
| **Instagram Paid Ads** | Targeted reach expansion | High for retargeting |

---

## 7. Audience Don'ts

- Do **not** speak to this audience in overly technical or clinical language
- Do **not** make before-and-after claims without evidence
- Do **not** use heavy sales pressure — this audience disengages from "buy now or miss out" tactics
- Do **not** assume they are already loyal customers; treat them as first-time discoverers
